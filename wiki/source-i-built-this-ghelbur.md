# I Built This for Myself. Then 1,374 Strangers Cloned It. (The AI Operator)

Origin story of [[obsidian-second-brain]] by [[eugeniu-ghelbur]] (The AI Operator, May 10, 2026).

## Key points

- Built to solve "institutional amnesia": two powerful tools (Claude, Obsidian) completely disconnected; every session starts from scratch; notes sit unused.
- 31 commands across four layers: remember, think with you, know you, pull knowledge in. Plus 4 scheduled agents that run while asleep.
- Traction metrics: 1,024 stars, 99 forks, 1,374 unique clones in 14 days, 5,910 unique visitors. Top referrer: Twitter. Second: Google.
- First user feedback: Cybersecurity Leader "personally impressed by the correlation engine within one hour" of migrating data.
- First external contribution: 695 lines of code adding a `/podcast` command, contributor also found a bug.
- Substack → GitHub conversion rate: 24%.

## Institutional amnesia framing

"Your team uses AI. Your team takes notes. The two don't talk. [...] By June, that person has switched roles or left. The customer insight is gone. Multiply that by fifty people on your team and you have institutional amnesia at scale."

This frames the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] not just as a personal tool but as an organizational pattern.
