# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; originator of the [[llm-wiki-pattern]] and the term [[vibe-coding]].

## Background

- Co-founder and research scientist at OpenAI (founding member)
- Director of AI at Tesla, led early development of Tesla Autopilot
- Founded Eureka Labs in 2024, focused on AI-native education
- Known as "the Godfather of Vibe Coding"

## LLM Wiki contribution

On April 2, 2026, Karpathy published a GitHub gist describing the LLM Wiki pattern — using an LLM to incrementally build and maintain a personal wiki from raw source documents, rather than re-deriving answers via [[rag]] at query time.

Published at: `gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f`

> "I think there is room here for an incredible new product." — on X, April 2026

Key framing: "Obsidian is the IDE, the LLM is the programmer, the wiki is the codebase."

At time of writing, Karpathy's own implementation used "a hacky collection of Python scripts" and he was still adding documents by hand, having the LLM do the filing and indexing.

## Influence

The gist triggered a wave of open-source implementations: [[obsidian-second-brain]] by [[eugeniu-ghelbur]] (1,024 stars), [[claude-obsidian]] by [[agrici-daniel]] (358 stars), and many others. Lex Fridman built a similar system generating "interactive podcasts." The pattern was covered by Techstrong.ai, HackerNoon, Medium, and multiple Substack newsletters within weeks.
