# Building a Second Brain: An Interview with Tiago Forte (Bullet Journal)

Interview with [[tiago-forte]] by Ryder Carroll (Bullet Journal, June 13, 2022), covering the [[second-brain]] concept, [[para-method]], and [[pkm]].

## Key points

- PKM vs. productivity distinction: productivity = actions; PKM = managing *content* (stories, evidence, examples, research).
- "Archive as debt forgiveness": move all old files to an Archive folder in one pass rather than organizing them. The search feature makes them findable.
- Organize information by *where it is going*, not where it came from. A graphic design project needs all its notes in one place.
- The brain excels at patterns, connections, stories, emotions — not memorizing details. Outsource details to software.
- "Crafting" notes: information presentation dramatically affects ability to absorb and act on it (UX/design lesson applied to notes).
- Don't seek the "perfect app" — the bottleneck is almost never the tool, it's the person's inner qualities.
- Analog + digital: Forte takes paper notes, photographs them weekly, uses handwriting recognition for search.

## Influences Forte cites

Sönke Ahrens (*How to Take Smart Notes*), Niklas Luhmann ([[zettelkasten]]), Anne-Laure Le Cunff (digital gardens), Andy Matuschak (evergreen notes), Annie Murphy Paul (*The Extended Mind*), Venkatesh Rao (Ribbonfarm).

## Ultimate purpose of PKM (Forte's view)

"To usher in the era of transhumanism — the fusion of man and machine. [...] PKM as the early stages of this historic transition."
