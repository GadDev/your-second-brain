# Tiago Forte

Author, productivity educator, and creator of the [[second-brain]] concept and the [[para-method]].

## Background

- Author of *Building a Second Brain* (2022) and *The PARA Method* (2023)
- Runs Forte Labs (fortelabs.co) and the BASB course (~5,000 students as of 2022)
- Hosts the Building a Second Brain podcast
- Background: worked at Apple Store coaching new Mac users, then Silicon Valley, then productivity coaching

## Key ideas

- The second brain as external memory: delegate factual recall to software so the biological brain can focus on creativity and connection.
- Organize information by *where it is going* (goal it serves), not by where it came from — the core insight of the [[para-method]].
- "Progressive Summarization" — distilling notes over multiple passes so the most useful information surfaces at a glance.
- The "Archive" as a way to escape data debt: move all old files into an archive in one pass rather than organizing them.

## Relation to LLM Wiki

[[andrej-karpathy]]'s approach is described as "borrowing from Tiago Forte's *Building a Second Brain*," but with a critical difference: in Forte's system, the *user* does all the indexing and maintenance. In Karpathy's system, the LLM does it.

> "In a sense, Karpathy is borrowing from author Tiago Forte's 2022 book 'Building a Second Brain,' which popularized the concept of building a knowledge base to store the details of everything someone learns. But with Karpathy's pattern, it is the LLM, not the user, that does all the indexing." — Techstrong.ai
