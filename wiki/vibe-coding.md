# Vibe Coding

A term coined by [[andrej-karpathy]] for the practice of rapidly building software by expressing intent to an AI agent and iterating on the output, rather than writing code manually.

## Origin

Karpathy described it in early 2025. The phrase spread quickly and became associated with his broader influence on developer practice — the idea that the bottleneck shifts from "can I write this code" to "can I communicate what I want."

## Relation to the LLM Wiki Pattern

The [[llm-wiki-pattern]] is framed in vibe-coding terms: [[obsidian]] is the IDE, the LLM is the programmer, the wiki is the codebase. Maintaining a wiki is a software problem — the schema (`CLAUDE.md`) is the spec, the slash commands are the build system, the agent is the developer.

Nick Spisak's implementation of the LLM Wiki on X is explicitly described as "one that doesn't involve writing any code," in line with the vibe-coding philosophy.
