# Eric J. Ma

Data scientist; author of a detailed account of using [[obsidian]] and AI coding agents for professional [[pkm]], managing 12 people across two teams.

## Background

- Data scientist and manager (12 reports, 2 teams, 2-4 projects each as of 2026)
- Has used Obsidian since ~2020
- Writes at ericmjl.github.io
- Publishes agent skills at github.com/ericmjl/skills

## PKM approach

Chose plain-text Obsidian in 2022, which became prescient when AI coding agents arrived — no migration needed, no conversion layer. Reduced knowledge management overhead from 30-40% of work time to under 10%.

**Note types:** monthly bullet journals, meeting notes (structured template), people notes ("dossiers" for everyone he works with), project notes ("control towers"), miscellaneous.

**Key practice:** documents vault structure in `AGENTS.md` so AI coding agents understand it. Links to a `HEARTBEAT.md` for periodic health checks.

**Ingestion:** meeting transcripts, PDFs, PowerPoints, Excel spreadsheets — all converted to plain text for the agent. Uses `python-docx`, `python-pptx`, `openpyxl` (not pandas — real spreadsheets are messy), and LibreOffice for image captioning on scanned docs.

**Ethical note:** keeps professional dossiers on colleagues but does not capture personal details without consent.
